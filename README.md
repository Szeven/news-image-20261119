# 19 November 2026: The World in One Day

An entry for the make.fun competition **news-image-20261119** ("Sum up 19
November's world news in one image", $250). One hand-built 1024×1024
infographic summing up Thursday 19 November 2026: six headline stories — the
first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen, Anthropic's
record-breaking $81.3bn IPO debut on Wall Street, Israel's rare ground
operation in central Gaza, the third strike this week on the Crimean Bridge,
Gadi Eisenkot being tapped to form Israel's next government, and the global
rollout of Grand Theft Auto VI — plus an "Also today" footer strip.

Every graphic is drawn from scratch as inline SVG; no photograph or anyone
else's artwork is used. See `NOTES.md` for the stories, sources, and how the
image was made. `main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (else `/output`).
