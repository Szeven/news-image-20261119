## Stories in the image

All events happened or were first reported on **Thursday 19 November 2026**.
Six headline panels + an "Also today" footer strip:

1. **DIPLOMACY — Three leaders, one table.** Trump, Xi and Putin meet in
   Shenzhen today — the first-ever trilateral summit of the world's three
   nuclear powers — at the APEC summit. Nuclear arms and the Iran war top the
   agenda; the New START treaty expired in February 2026 and the US expects
   no agreement.
2. **TECH · MARKETS — Biggest IPO in history.** Anthropic debuts on Wall
   Street today (Nasdaq: ANT): 980m shares at $83 raise $81.3bn — passing
   SpaceX's $75bn record — valuing the Claude maker at about $1.5tn, plus a
   $26bn private placement.
3. **CONFLICT — Gaza ground operation.** Israeli troops launch a rare ground
   operation in central Gaza, beyond the ceasefire line, as displaced families
   flee their tents and flooding deepens the crisis; Australia, Canada, Italy
   and the UK call on Israel to let aid reach flooded Gaza.
4. **WAR — Crimea bridge hit again.** A third strike this week halts traffic
   on the Kerch bridge in the early hours of Thursday; a 227-metre road
   section collapsed into the sea on Tuesday 17 November. The rail link still
   runs.
5. **POLITICS — Eisenkot to govern.** Israel's president asks former army
   chief Gadi Eisenkot to try to form the next government — choosing him over
   sitting prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu.
6. **CULTURE — Vice City opens its doors.** Grand Theft Auto VI begins its
   global rollout at midnight local time, 13 years after GTA V — Lucia and
   Jason return to Vice City; pre-orders already top $1bn.

Footer — **Also today:** The Hague delivers its landmark Rohingya genocide
verdict on Myanmar; Van Gogh's "Châtaigniers en fleurs" sells for a record
$190.4m at Sotheby's; Cyclone Akvan bears down on India's east coast.

## How the image was made

- `page.html` is a hand-written HTML/CSS infographic (1024×1024): navy masthead
  with the date **19 NOVEMBER 2026** prominent, a 2×3 grid of story cards, each
  with a colour-coded kicker, a short headline, a 1–2 line deck, a bold key-fact
  line, a source line and a simple hand-drawn inline-SVG icon. No photograph or
  anyone else's artwork is used; every graphic is drawn from scratch.
- Rendered with the local Firefox tarball (headless, no network):
  `/home/hatch/workspace/firefox/firefox --headless --screenshot <abs path>/entry.png --window-size=1024,1024 file:///home/hatch/workspace/makefun/news-image-20261119/page.html`
- The rendered PNG was visually checked: date, all six headlines, key facts and
  the footer are legible, no garbled text, no overlap, no clipping.
- `main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (else `/output`), so both grader runs write identical
  bytes. No network at runtime.

## How to run / verify

```sh
cd /home/hatch/workspace/makefun/news-image-20261119
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts   # exit 0
ls -l out/entry.png                                    # valid PNG, 1024x1024, <= 1 MiB
```
